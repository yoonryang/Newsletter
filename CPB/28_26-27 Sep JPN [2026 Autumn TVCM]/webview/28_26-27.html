"Let's Pukatsu" is Back for Japan's Autumn Season

Welcome to the latest news from the California Prune Board! In this edition, we are delighted to remind you our California Prunes "Pukatsu" CM campaign is on live again from next month! TV spot and digital advertising are broadcasted throughout multimedia.

“Lets Pukatsu” TVCM runs from 1st October through 30th November 2026 and include an upweighted campaign of just over 8 weeks of TV campaign

Free publicity is secured during October and November

Digital campaign starts from October 1st to November 30th

Supported with over 50 POS materials
Check it now, 2026 Fall TVCM & Free Publicity schedules are published!

Campaign will start on 1st October through 30th November 2026 and include an upweighted campaign of just over 8 weeks of TV giving our Japanese trade stakeholders a substantial period for instore sales promotional opportunities.

  • TVCM will achieve over 4,700 GRP's (Gross Rating Points)
  • We will be covering 7 regions, averaging over 670 GRPs per region
  • Our TVCM campaign will reach over 55 million Japanese consumers(total TV audience), with our specific target audience of MF4-69 reaching over 40 million consumers
  • We are focussing on two TV channels per region allowing us to reach new audiences
  • Additional free TV publicity has been secured

You can visit our new page to download schedules of TVCM and Free Publicity right now with New Password : CAprunes from the link below.

    Download TVCM & Free Publicity Schedule
The 2026 autumn Digital campaign continues until the end November!

Whilst the TVCM campaign reaches existing users, the digital campaign will reach a younger audience of new consumers, building tomorrows demand for California Prunes.

Complementing our TVCM flight, our digital campaign kicks off on October 1st and will continue to November 30th. This digital campaign will feature on the following platforms: Google, Yahoo, Amazon, Rakuten, Taboola,Teads, Instagram, YouTube, and TVer, we will aim to achieve over 75M impressions.
