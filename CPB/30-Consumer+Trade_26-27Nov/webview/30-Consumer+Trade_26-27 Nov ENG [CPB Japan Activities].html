California Prunes in the Spotlight

From inspiring the next generation of chefs to connecting with consumers through lifestyle and wellness campaigns, California Prunes are reaching audiences in new and exciting ways across Japan. Here's a look at some of the latest activity.
Tomorrow's Chefs Put Prunes to the Test

What happens when aspiring chefs are given California Prune ingredients and the freedom to get creative? At Murakawa Gakuen's third annual recipe contest, students across four campuses rose to the challenge with original sweet and savoury dishes. CPB seminars introduced students to the taste, nutrition and versatility of California Prunes before the competition.

From the preliminary rounds, 38 teams went through to campus contests and five recipes took Grand Prix honours. The winning creations are set to appear on CPB Japan's website, with plans to showcase them at Green Expo 2027 and Daimaru Umeda.
A Little More Personality: Meet Sunny and Otto

Say hello to Sunny and Otto, the characters who are continuing to add a playful twist to California Prunes' social content. From Japanese seasonal celebrations to Halloween and Christmas, they're popping up in posts that make the brand feel friendly and familiar. They'll have a starring role in the next consumer campaign, too.

Sweet or Savoury? Japan Gets to Choose

A little festive debate is coming to Instagram this December: are California Prunes better sweet or savoury? The “Year-End Treat Yourself” campaign invites consumers to vote, comment and follow for the chance to win branded gifts. For the first time, Sunny and Otto merchandise will be among the prizes, while a separate promotion on X will invite people to follow and repost for a chance to win digital gift cards. It's a fun way to get consumers talking about all the ways they can enjoy prunes.
From Work Breaks to Café Discoveries

California Prunes are finding their way into the moments that make up a busy day. Two founders of the Cocochii Wellness Community are sharing Instagram Reels featuring prunes as a convenient snack around workouts and work breaks. Meanwhile, Tokyo-based gourmet creators are set to introduce two café collaborations to their followers: Tokyo Gen Z Gourmet will feature MUUN Seoul's California Prune cheese bagel in November, followed by Aya Gourmet's look at the prune, orange and chocolate doughnut at UNI DONUTS in December.
Wellness, With a California Prune Twist

Where better to discover a new everyday snack than in a setting dedicated to feeling good? In December, California Prune samples will reach visitors at spas and hot springs in locations including Tokyo, Osaka and Chiba. A linked information page will highlight gut and bone health, with a short survey helping gauge awareness and purchase interest. In January, the focus shifts to running stations, where active consumers can try California Prunes around their fitness routines. Two different settings, one simple idea: put the fruit into people's hands when it feels relevant.
